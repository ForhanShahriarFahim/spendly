Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

01 Data We Collect

To run your Spendly account, we collect only what the service needs:

  • Account details such as your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and notes.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type and login timestamps, used for security.

02 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide Spendly's features: recording expenses, showing summaries and spending breakdowns, and keeping your account secure. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

03 Data Storage

Your information is stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may delete your account and its data at any time.

04 Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third parties. Pages may load resources such as web fonts from external providers, which may receive standard request information like your IP address. If we add other third party services in the future, this policy will be updated to describe them.

05 Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to access or delete your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
